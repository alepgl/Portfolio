Sei già a metà strada

Many hesitate to act, fearing they might make mistakes. But what if you were already halfway there?

The "Sei già a metà strada" campaign encourages people to overcome doubt and take action.

Even basic knowledge and intuition can make a difference—you likely know more than you think. With the right mindset, you can turn hesitation into confidence and make a real impact.
